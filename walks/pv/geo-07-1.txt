@@ g71Regular 1000
title: Regular polygon angles
exterior: 360° ÷ 40 = 9° || In a regular polygon, all the exterior angles are equal. They add to 360, so each is 360 divided by 40: 9 degrees.
interior: 180° − 9° = 171° || Each interior angle forms a linear pair with an exterior angle, so it's 180 minus 9: 171 degrees.
