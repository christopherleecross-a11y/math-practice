title: Find δ for a given ε
|(−3x + 6) − 0| = |−3(x − 2)| || Start with the distance between f of x and the limit, and factor so that x minus 2 appears.
= 3|x − 2| || The absolute value of negative 3 is 3.
3|x − 2| < 0.012 → |x − 2| < 0.012/3 || We need 3 times the absolute value of x minus 2 to be less than 0.012. Divide by 3.
δ = 0.004 || So the largest delta that works is 0.004. In general, for a line with slope m, delta is epsilon divided by the absolute value of m.
