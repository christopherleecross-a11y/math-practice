title: Geometric mean of two numbers
geometric mean of a and b = √(ab) || The geometric mean of two numbers is the square root of their product.
√(33 · 42) = √1386 || 33 times 42 is 1386.
1386 = 9 · 154 → 3√154 || 1386 is 9 times 154, and the square root of 9 is 3. So it's 3 root 154.
