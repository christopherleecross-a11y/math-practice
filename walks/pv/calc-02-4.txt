title: The Product Rule
(fg)′ = f′g + fg′ || Product Rule: the derivative of the first times the second, plus the first times the derivative of the second.
f′(x) = 2(x² + 4) + (2x + 4)(2x) || The derivative of 2 x plus 4 is 2. The derivative of x squared plus 4 is 2 x.
f′(−2) = 2(8) + (0)(−4) = 16 || Put in negative 2: 2 times 8, plus 0 times negative 4. That's 16.

@@ cd24Quotient 1000
title: The Quotient Rule
(f/g)′ = (f′g − fg′)/g² || Quotient Rule: bottom times derivative of the top, minus top times derivative of the bottom, all over the bottom squared.
f′(x) = (3(x + 4) − (3x + 4)(1))/(x + 4)² || The top's derivative is 3 and the bottom's derivative is 1.
= (3x + 12 − 3x − 4)/(x + 4)² = 8/(x + 4)² || Simplify the numerator: 8.
f′(−2) = 8/(2)² = 2 || Put in negative 2: 8 over 4 is 2.
