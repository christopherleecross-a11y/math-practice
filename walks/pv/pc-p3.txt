title: Add and subtract functions
(f + g)(x) = (x² + x − 5) + (x − 4) || To add functions, add their expressions.
= x² + 2x − 9 || Combine like terms: x plus x is 2 x, and negative 5 minus 4 is negative 9.
(f − g)(x) = (x² + x − 5) − (x − 4) = x² + x − 5 − x + 4 || To subtract, put g in parentheses and change the sign of every term in it.
= x² − 1 || The x terms cancel, and negative 5 plus 4 is negative 1.
(f/g)(x): g(x) ≠ 0 → x ≠ 4 || If your version also asks for f over g, its domain leaves out where g is 0: x can't be 4.

@@ p9Compose 1000
title: Composition of functions
(f ∘ g)(x) = f(g(x)) = f(x² − 2) || f of g means put g inside f.
= −4(x² − 2) = −4x² + 8 || f multiplies its input by negative 4.
(g ∘ f)(x) = g(−4x) = (−4x)² − 2 = 16x² − 2 || g of f puts negative 4 x into g. Square it, then subtract 2. Order matters: this is different.
(g ∘ g)(x) = (x² − 2)² − 2 = x⁴ − 4x² + 2 || g of g puts g into itself. Expand x squared minus 2, squared: x to the 4th minus 4 x squared plus 4. Then subtract 2.

@@ p9CompDomain 1001
kind: √
title: Composition and domain: a square root
f ∘ g = √(x² + 2) → domain: all real numbers || f of g puts x squared into the square root. x squared plus 2 is always positive, so every x works.
g ∘ f = (√(x + 2))² = x + 2 || g of f squares the square root, which gives x plus 2.
but x must be in the domain of f: x ≥ −2 → [−2, ∞) || But x has to go into f first, and f needs x plus 2 to be at least 0. So the domain is negative 2 to infinity, even though x plus 2 looks like it works everywhere.

@@ p9CompDomain 1015
kind: ∛
title: Composition and domain: a cube root
f ∘ g = ∛((x³ + 6) − 3) = ∛(x³ + 3) || f of g puts x cubed plus 6 into f.
g ∘ f = (∛(x − 3))³ + 6 = x − 3 + 6 = x + 3 || g of f cubes the cube root, which undoes it.
both domains: all real numbers || Cube roots work for every real number, so both domains are all real numbers.

g ∘ f = |x| + 4 || g of f adds 4 to the absolute value of x.
both domains: all real numbers || Nothing is ever undefined, so both domains are all real numbers.

title: Composition and domain: a reciprocal
f ∘ g = 1/(x + 8) → x ≠ −8 || f of g puts x plus 8 in the denominator. It can't be 0, so x can't be negative 8.
g ∘ f = 1/x + 8 → x ≠ 0 || g of f adds 8 to 1 over x. Now x can't be 0.
(−∞, −8) ∪ (−8, ∞) and (−∞, 0) ∪ (0, ∞) || Write each domain in interval notation.

title: Composition and domain: a rational function
f ∘ g = 4/((x + 1)² − 1) = 4/(x² + 2x) || f of g puts x plus 1 into f. x plus 1 squared minus 1 simplifies to x squared plus 2 x.
x² + 2x = x(x + 2) ≠ 0 → x ≠ 0, −2 || The denominator factors as x times x plus 2, so x can't be 0 or negative 2.
