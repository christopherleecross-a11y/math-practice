@@ g113Area 1023
kind: apothem
title: Area of a regular polygon from the apothem
A = ½aP || The area of a regular polygon is one half times the apothem times the perimeter. You know the apothem, so you need the perimeter.
360° ÷ 8 = 45°, half is 22.5° || Each central angle is 45 degrees. The apothem splits it, making a right triangle with a 22.5 degree angle at the center.
r = 7 ÷ cos 22.5° ≈ 7.58 || In that right triangle the apothem is the adjacent side and the radius is the hypotenuse. So r is 7 divided by cosine of 22.5: about 7.58.
s = 2 · 7.58 · sin 22.5° ≈ 5.80, P ≈ 8 · 5.80 ≈ 46.39 || Half a side is r times sine of 22.5, so a full side is about 5.80. Eight sides make about 46.39.
A ≈ ½ · 7 · 46.39 ≈ 162.4 square units || Area is one half times 7 times 46.39: about 162.4 square units.
