title: The derivative of ln u
(ln u)′ = u′/u || The derivative of the natural log of u is u prime over u.
f′(x) = 6x/(3x² + 5) || Here u is 3 x squared plus 5, so u prime is 6 x.
f′(1) = 6/8 = 3/4 || At 1: 6 over 8, which is 3 fourths.

@@ cc34LnQuot 1000
title: The derivative of ln of a quotient
ln(A/B) = ln A − ln B || Use a log property first: the log of a quotient is a difference of logs. It makes the derivative much easier.
f(x) = ln(2x + 5) − ln(x + 5) || Rewrite f.
f′(x) = 2/(2x + 5) − 1/(x + 5) || Each piece is u prime over u.
f′(0) = 2/5 − 1/5 = 1/5 || At 0: 2 fifths minus 1 fifth is 1 fifth.
