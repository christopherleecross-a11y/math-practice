title: The derivative from the definition: a quadratic
f(−3) = −3(9) − 2(−3) + 2 = −19 || The definition needs f of c. Here c is negative 3: f of negative 3 is negative 27 plus 6 plus 2, which is negative 19.
f(x) − f(−3) = −3x² − 2x + 21 = (x + 3)(−3(x − 3) − 2) || Subtract: negative 3 x squared minus 2 x plus 21. It has to have a factor of x plus 3, so factor it out.

@@ cd21Def 1002

f(x) − f(2) = −1/x + 1/2 = (x − 2)/(2x) || Find f of x minus f of 2. f of 2 is negative one half. Use a common denominator of 2 x: the top becomes x minus 2.
÷ (x − 2): 1/(2x) || Divide by x minus 2. It cancels, leaving 1 over 2 x.
→ 1/(2 · 2) = 1/4 || Let x approach 2: 1 over 4. So f prime of 2 is one fourth.
