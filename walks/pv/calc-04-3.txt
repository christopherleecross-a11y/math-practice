@@ cg43Ladder 1000

x² + y² = 17² → 2x·dx/dt + 2y·dy/dt = 0 || The ladder, wall, and ground form a right triangle. Differentiate with respect to time.
x = 8 → y = √(289 − 64) = 15 || Find y at this moment: 15.
