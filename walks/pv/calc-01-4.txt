title: The Squeeze Theorem
6x − 5 → 6(3) − 5 = 13 || f is trapped between two functions. Find the limit of the bottom one: 6 times 3 minus 5 is 13.
x² + 4 → 9 + 4 = 13 || Now the top one: 9 plus 4 is 13.
both bounds → 13, so f(x) → 13 || Both bounds approach 13, and f is squeezed between them, so f has to approach 13 too.
lim(x→3) f(x) = 13 || That's the Squeeze Theorem.
