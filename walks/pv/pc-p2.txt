title: Name a parent function
f(x) = x → identity || f of x equals x is the identity function: the output is the same as the input. It's also linear, but identity is more specific.
c → constant;  mx + b → linear;  x² → squaring;  x³ → cubic || Others to know: a plain number is the constant function, m x plus b is linear, x squared is the squaring function, and x cubed is cubic.
√x → square root;  1/x → reciprocal;  |x| → absolute value;  ⟦x⟧ → greatest integer || Root x is the square root function, 1 over x is the reciprocal function, bars mean absolute value, and double brackets mean the greatest integer function.

@@ p7Step 1003
title: Evaluate a greatest integer function
⟦x⟧ = the greatest integer ≤ x || The greatest integer of a number is the integer at or just below it. Always round down.
f(21/5): 21/5 + 4 = 4.2 + 4 = 8.2 → ⟦8.2⟧ = 8 || 21 fifths is 4.2. Add 4: 8.2. Rounding down gives 8.
f(21/5) = −8 + 6 = −2 || Then finish: negative 8 plus 6 is negative 2.
f(−4): ⟦0⟧ = 0 → f(−4) = 6 || At negative 4, the inside is 0, so f is 0 plus 6, which is 6.
careful with negatives: ⟦−3.1⟧ = −4 || With negatives, rounding down goes away from 0: the greatest integer of negative 3.1 is negative 4.
