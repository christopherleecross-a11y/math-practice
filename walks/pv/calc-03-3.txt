g′(−7) = 1/f′(g(−7)) || The derivative of an inverse: g prime of a is 1 over f prime of g of a.
f(−1) = −1 − 2 − 4 = −7 → g(−7) = −1 || First find g of negative 7: which x makes f of x equal negative 7? Try small numbers: f of negative 1 is negative 7.
f′(x) = 3x² + 2 → f′(−1) = 5 || The derivative of f is 3 x squared plus 2. At negative 1, that's 5.
g′(−7) = 1/5 || So g prime of negative 7 is one fifth.
