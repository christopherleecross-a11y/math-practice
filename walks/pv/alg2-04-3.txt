title: Long division by x + a
8x³ ÷ x = 8x² → 8x²(x + 4) = 8x³ + 32x² || Divide the first term by x: 8 x squared. Multiply it by x plus 4.
38x² − 32x² = 6x² → bring down 17x || Subtract, then bring down the next term: 6 x squared plus 17 x.
6x(x + 4) = 6x² + 24x → −7x − 39 || Divide again: 6 x. Multiply and subtract: 17 x minus 24 x is negative 7 x. Bring down negative 39.
−7(x + 4) = −7x − 28 → remainder −11 || Divide: negative 7. Multiply and subtract: negative 39 plus 28 is negative 11.
8x² + 6x − 7 − 11/(x + 4) || The quotient is 8 x squared plus 6 x minus 7, with a remainder of negative 11.

@@ b43Synth 1004
kind: \(−?\d*[a-z]²
title: Synthetic division
x + 4 → k = −4; coefficients 3, 13, −11 || Dividing by x plus 4 means k is negative 4. Write the coefficients.
3 → 13 + (−12) = 1 → −11 + (−4) = −15 || Bring down 3. Multiply by negative 4 and add to the next column, each time.
quotient 3x + 1, remainder −15 || The last number is the remainder. The others are the quotient's coefficients, one degree lower.

quotient 4x² − 6x + 3, remainder 0 || The remainder is 0, so x minus 1 divides evenly.
