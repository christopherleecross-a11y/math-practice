title: Implicit differentiation with an xy term
d/dx: 4x + 2(y + x·y′) + 6y·y′ = 0 || Differentiate every term with respect to x. Every y term gets a y prime. 2 x y needs the Product Rule: y plus x y prime.
y′(2x + 6y) = −(4x + 2y) || Collect the y prime terms on one side.
y′ = −(4x + 2y)/(2x + 6y) || Divide to solve for y prime.
at (3, −3): y′ = −(12 − 6)/(6 − 18) = −6/(−12) = 1/2 || Put in x equals 3 and y equals negative 3: one half.

@@ cc32Rational 1000
title: The Power Rule with a rational exponent
f′(x) = 6 · (−1/2)x^(−3/2) = −3x^(−3/2) || The Power Rule works for any exponent: bring down negative one half, and subtract 1 from the exponent.
9^(−3/2) = 1/(√9)³ = 1/27 || 9 to the negative 3 halves: the square root of 9 is 3, cubed is 27, and the negative makes it 1 over 27.
f′(9) = −3/27 = −1/9 || Negative 3 over 27 is negative 1 ninth.

title: A second derivative by implicit differentiation
y′ = −x/y || First derivative of x squared plus y squared equals 8: y prime is negative x over y.
y″ = −(y − x·y′)/y² || Quotient Rule on negative x over y.
= −(y² + x²)/y³ = −8/y³ || Substitute y prime and simplify. x squared plus y squared is 8.
at y = 2: −8/8 = −1 || At the point, y is 2: negative 1.
