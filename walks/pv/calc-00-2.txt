title: Zeros, multiplicity, and crossing or touching
(x + 3) → zero −3, multiplicity 1 || Each factor gives a zero. x plus 3 gives negative 3, and it appears once: multiplicity 1.
(x + 4)² → zero −4, multiplicity 2 || x plus 4 squared gives negative 4 with multiplicity 2.
odd → crosses;  even → touches || An odd multiplicity means the graph crosses the x-axis. An even multiplicity means it touches and turns around.
−4 (multiplicity 2, touches);  −3 (multiplicity 1, crosses) || So at negative 4 it touches, and at negative 3 it crosses.

@@ cpRational 1000
title: Domain and intercepts of a rational function
x² − 7x + 10 = (x − 2)(x − 5) → x ≠ 2, 5 || Factor the denominator. It's 0 at 2 and 5, so those are left out of the domain.
numerator 3(x − 4) = 0 → x = 4 → x-intercept (4, 0) || The graph crosses the x-axis where the numerator is 0 and the denominator isn't: x equals 4.
R(0) = 3(−4)/10 = −12/10 = −6/5 → y-intercept (0, −6/5) || For the y-intercept, put in 0: negative 12 over 10, which is negative 6 fifths.
