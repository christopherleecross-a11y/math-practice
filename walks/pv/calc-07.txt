title: Order and linearity of a differential equation
Order = highest derivative that appears || The order of a differential equation is the highest derivative in it.
y″ + sin y = 0 → order 2 || Here the highest derivative is y double prime, so the order is 2.
Linear: y, y′, y″ only to the first power, times functions of x || A linear equation has y and its derivatives only to the first power, never inside another function.
sin y → nonlinear || Sine of y puts y inside a function, so this equation is nonlinear. Order 2, nonlinear.
