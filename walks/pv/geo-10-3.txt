@@ g103Radius 1000
title: Radius from a chord
the ⊥ from the center bisects the chord: half = 16 ÷ 2 = 8 || The perpendicular from the center to a chord cuts it in half: 8 on each side.
right triangle: legs 8 and 15, hypotenuse r || That makes a right triangle with legs 8 and 15. The hypotenuse is a radius.
r² = 64 + 225 = 289 → r = 17 || r squared is 289, so r is 17.
