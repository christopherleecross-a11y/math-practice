title: Derivatives of sine, cosine, and tangent
(sin x)′ = cos x,  (cos x)′ = −sin x,  (tan x)′ = sec²x || The three derivatives to know: sine gives cosine, cosine gives negative sine, and tangent gives secant squared.
f′(x) = 2sec²x + cos x || So 2 tangent x becomes 2 secant squared x, and sine x becomes cosine x.
sec(7π/4) = √2 → sec² = 2;  cos(7π/4) = √2/2 || At 7 pi over 4, cosine is root 2 over 2, so secant is root 2 and secant squared is 2.
f′(7π/4) = 2(2) + √2/2 = 4 + √2/2 || So f prime is 4 plus root 2 over 2.

f′(x) = (1)sin x + x cos x || Product Rule: the derivative of x is 1, and the derivative of sine x is cosine x.
f′(π) = sin π + π cos π = 0 + π(−1) || At pi, sine is 0 and cosine is negative 1.

title: Product Rule: x cos x
f′(x) = (1)cos x + x(−sin x) = cos x − x sin x || Product Rule: the derivative of cosine x is negative sine x.
f′(π/6) = √3/2 − (π/6)(1/2) || At pi over 6, cosine is root 3 over 2 and sine is one half.
= √3/2 − π/12 || Pi over 6 times one half is pi over 12.
