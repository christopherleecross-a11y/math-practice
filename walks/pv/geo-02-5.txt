title: Properties of congruence
Reflexive: E̅H̅ ≅ E̅H̅ || The congruence properties match the equality properties. Reflexive: a segment or angle is congruent to itself.
Symmetric: if A̅B̅ ≅ C̅D̅, then C̅D̅ ≅ A̅B̅ || Symmetric: you can switch the order.
Transitive: if A̅B̅ ≅ C̅D̅ and C̅D̅ ≅ E̅F̅, then A̅B̅ ≅ E̅F̅ || Transitive: two congruence statements that share a middle piece link together.
E̅H̅ ≅ E̅H̅ → Reflexive Property of Segment Congruence || Here, segment E H is congruent to itself. It's a segment, so the answer is the Reflexive Property of Segment Congruence.
