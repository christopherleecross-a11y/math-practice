@@ t2Period 1007
title: Use the period to evaluate
cos has period 2π || Cosine repeats every 2 pi, so you can subtract 2 pi without changing the value.
7π/3 − 2π = 7π/3 − 6π/3 = π/3 || Write 2 pi as 6 pi over 3 and subtract.
cos(7π/3) = cos(π/3) = 1/2 || Cosine of pi over 3 is one half.

@@ t5AmpPeriod 1003
title: Amplitude and period
y = a sin bx: amplitude = |a|, period = 2π/b || For a sine or cosine graph, the amplitude is the absolute value of a, and the period is 2 pi over b.
amplitude = |2| = 2 || Here a is 2.
b = π/4 → period = 2π/(π/4) = 2π · 4/π = 8 || b is pi over 4. Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal: 8.

@@ t5WriteEq 1001
title: Write a sine or cosine equation
a = amplitude = 2 || a is the amplitude: 2.
b = 2π/period = 2π/(2π/3) = 3 || b is 2 pi over the period: 3.
c = b · (phase shift) = 3 · π/2 = 3π/2 || The phase shift is c over b, so c is b times the phase shift. Shifting right makes c positive.
d = 4 → y = 2 sin(3x − 3π/2) + 4 || d is the vertical shift, 4 up. Put it all together.

@@ t5FindABC 1000
title: Find a, b, and c from a graph
amplitude 3 → a = 3 || The graph goes up 3 and down 3 from the middle, so a is 3.
period 4π → b = 2π/4π = 1/2 || One full cycle is 4 pi long, so b is 2 pi over 4 pi: one half.
