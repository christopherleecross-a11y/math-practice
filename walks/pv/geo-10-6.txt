title: Segments of chords
part × part = part × part || Segments of Chords Theorem: when two chords cross, the two parts of one chord multiply to the same number as the two parts of the other.
4 · 4 = 4 · x || One chord is split into 4 and 4. The other is split into 4 and x.
16 = 4x → x = 4 || 16 equals 4 x, so x is 4.

@@ g106Secants 1000
title: Segments of secants
outside part × whole secant = outside part × whole secant || Segments of Secants Theorem: for each secant from the outside point, multiply the outside part by the whole secant. The products are equal.
4 · (4 + 3) = x · (x + 12) || The top secant has outside part 4 and whole length 4 plus 3, which is 7. The bottom has outside part x and whole length x plus 12.
28 = x² + 12x → x² + 12x − 28 = 0 || Expand and move everything to one side.
(x + 14)(x − 2) = 0 → x = 2 || Factor: x is 2 or negative 14. A length is positive, so x is 2.

@@ g106Tangent 1000
title: A secant and a tangent
tangent² = outside part × whole secant || When a tangent and a secant come from the same point, the tangent squared equals the outside part of the secant times the whole secant.
15² = 9 · (9 + x) || The tangent is 15. The secant's outside part is 9, and the whole secant is 9 plus x.
225 = 81 + 9x → 144 = 9x → x = 16 || Distribute, subtract 81, and divide by 9: x is 16.
