kind: Find the surface area of the regular square pyramid
title: Surface area of a pyramid from its slant height
S = B + ½Pℓ || A pyramid's surface area is its base plus its triangle faces. The faces add up to one half times the base perimeter times the slant height, l.
B = 4² = 16, P = 4(4) = 16 || The base is a 4 by 4 square: B is 16 and the perimeter is 16.
S = 16 + ½(16)(10) = 16 + 80 = 96 in.² || The slant height is 10. One half of 16 times 10 is 80. Add the base: 96 square inches.

title: Surface area of a cone from its slant height
S = πr² + πrℓ || A cone's surface area is its circle base, pi r squared, plus its curved side, pi r l, where l is the slant height.
π(3)² + π(3)(12) = 9π + 36π = 45π || The radius is 3 and the slant height is 12: 9 pi plus 36 pi is 45 pi.
≈ 141.37 in.² || About 141.37 square inches.
