title: Scale factor of similar polygons
△RST ~ △EFG: S ↔ F, T ↔ G || Match a pair of corresponding sides using the order of the letters. S T in the first triangle matches F G in the second.
from △RST to △EFG: new ÷ original = FG/ST || The scale factor from the first triangle to the second is the second's side over the first's.
24/16 = 3/2 || F G is 24 and S T is 16. 24 over 16 is 3 halves.

△CDE ~ △QRS: C ↔ Q, D ↔ R, E ↔ S || Pair up the vertices in order.
CD/QR = DE/RS = EC/SQ || Each side of the first over its matching side of the second: C D over Q R, D E over R S, E C over S Q. All these ratios are equal.
