vertex: L || An angle can be named three ways. First, find the vertex, where the two rays start. Here it's L.
∠L || You can name it by its vertex alone: angle L. That works when only one angle is at that vertex.
∠2 || You can name it by the number inside it: angle 2.
∠HLQ or ∠QLH || Or by three points: one on each ray, with the vertex in the middle. Angle H L Q, or angle Q L H.
∠HLQ, ∠QLH, ∠L || So a correct list is angle H L Q, angle Q L H, angle L. A name like angle L H Q is wrong, because the vertex isn't in the middle.
