title: The tangent of an angle
tan = opposite/adjacent || Tangent is the opposite side over the adjacent side.
from L: opposite = 15 (QH), adjacent = 8 (LH) || Stand at angle L. The side across from it is Q H, 15. The leg next to it, not the hypotenuse, is L H, 8.
tan L = 15/8 = 1.8750 || So tangent L is 15 over 8, which is 1.875.

title: Find a side with tangent: x opposite
tan 43° = opposite/adjacent = x/24 || x is across from the 43 degree angle and 24 is next to it. Tangent uses those two.
x = 24 tan 43° ≈ 22.4 || Multiply both sides by 24: x is 24 times tangent 43, about 22.4.

title: Find a side with tangent: x adjacent
tan 28° = 11/x || 11 is across from the 28 degree angle and x is next to it.
x · tan 28° = 11 → x = 11/tan 28° ≈ 20.7 || Multiply both sides by x, then divide by tangent 28: x is about 20.7. When x is on the bottom, you divide.

@@ g94Word 1000
title: Tangent in real life: angle of elevation
the height is opposite 73°; 250 ft is adjacent || Draw a right triangle: the height of the building is across from the 73 degree angle, and your distance, 250 feet, is next to it.
tan 73° = h/250 || So tangent 73 equals h over 250.
h = 250 tan 73° ≈ 818 ft || Multiply by 250: about 818 feet.

@@ g94Challenge 1000
title: Challenge: a building between two observers
x = h/tan 53°;  500 − x = h/tan 48° || Let h be the height and x the first person's distance. Tangent gives x equals h over tangent 53, and the other distance, 500 minus x, equals h over tangent 48.
add: 500 = h(1/tan 53° + 1/tan 48°) || Add the two distances: they make 500.
h ≈ 302.3 ft || Solve for h: about 302.3 feet.
x = 302.3/tan 53° ≈ 227.8 ft || The first person is about 227.8 feet from the base.
