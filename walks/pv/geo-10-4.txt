@@ g104Quad 1000
title: Inscribed quadrilaterals
opposite angles of an inscribed quadrilateral are supplementary || When a quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle, its opposite angles add to 180.
5x + 100 = 180 → x = 16 || The 5 x angle is across from 100 degrees: 5 x is 80, so x is 16.
3y + 90 = 180 → y = 30 || The 3 y angle is across from 90 degrees: 3 y is 90, so y is 30.

title: Can every one be inscribed in a circle?
a quadrilateral can be inscribed ⇔ opposite angles are supplementary || A quadrilateral fits in a circle exactly when its opposite angles add to 180. Every triangle can be inscribed.
always: rectangles, squares, isosceles trapezoids || Rectangles and squares have all right angles, so they always work. Isosceles trapezoids do too, because their base angles match.
not always: rhombuses, parallelograms, kites || Most rhombuses, parallelograms, and kites have opposite angles that don't add to 180.
kite → No || So not every kite can be inscribed. Only kites with two opposite right angles can.
