title: Scale factor: reduction or enlargement
k = CP′/CP || The scale factor is the image distance divided by the original distance: C P prime over C P.
k = 27/15 = 9/5 || 27 over 15 simplifies to 9 fifths.
k > 1 → enlargement;  0 < k < 1 → reduction || If k is greater than 1, the image is bigger: an enlargement. If k is between 0 and 1, it's a reduction.
9/5 > 1 → an enlargement || 9 fifths is more than 1, so this is an enlargement.
