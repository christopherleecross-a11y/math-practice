@@ g124Prism 1000
kind: triangular prism
title: Volume of a triangular prism
V = Bh || The volume of a prism is the area of its base, B, times its height.
B = ½(9)(12) = 54 || The base is a right triangle with legs 9 and 12: one half times 9 times 12 is 54.
V = 54 · 2 = 108 yd³ || The prism is 2 long, so the volume is 108 cubic yards.

@@ g124Cyl 1000
title: Volume of a cylinder
V = πr²h || A cylinder is like a prism with a circle base: base area pi r squared, times the height.
r = 26 ÷ 2 = 13 || The diameter is 26, so the radius is 13.
V = π(13)²(18) = 3042π ≈ 9556.72 yd³ || 169 times 18 is 3042. So the volume is 3042 pi, about 9556.72 cubic yards.
