title: Density from mass and volume
density = mass ÷ volume || Density is how much mass is packed into each unit of volume: mass divided by volume.
283 ÷ 178 ≈ 1.59 g/cm³ || 283 grams divided by 178 cubic centimeters is about 1.59 grams per cubic centimeter.
not volume ÷ mass || Keep the order: grams on top, because the units are grams per cubic centimeter.
