title: Surface area of a triangular prism
S = 2B + Ph || The surface area of a prism is the two bases, 2 B, plus the side faces, which unroll into a rectangle: perimeter of the base times the height.
B = ½(9)(12) = 54 || The bases are right triangles with legs 9 and 12, so each base is one half times 9 times 12: 54.
P = 9 + 12 + 15 = 36, h = 3 || The perimeter of the triangle is 36, and the prism's height is 3.
S = 2(54) + 36(3) = 216 yd² || 2 times 54 is 108, and 36 times 3 is 108. Together, 216 square yards.
