title: Measuring with a ruler
L is at 0.4 cm. || Read where each endpoint lines up on the ruler. L lines up with 0.4 centimeters.
Q is at 2.6 cm. || Q lines up with 2.6 centimeters.
LQ = |2.6 − 0.4| || The length is the distance between the two readings: the absolute value of their difference.
= 2.2 cm || 2.6 minus 0.4 is 2.2. So L Q is 2.2 centimeters.

@@ g12Congruent 1000
title: Are the segments congruent?
A(5, 3), B(5, 7), C(6, 0), D(8, 0) || Plot the four points. Congruent segments have the same length, so find both lengths.
A and B have the same x → vertical: AB = |7 − 3| = 4 || A and B have the same x coordinate, so A B is vertical. Its length is the difference of the y values: 7 minus 3 is 4.
C and D have the same y → horizontal: CD = |8 − 6| = 2 || C and D have the same y coordinate, so C D is horizontal. Its length is 8 minus 6, which is 2.
4 ≠ 2 → not congruent || The lengths are different, so the segments are not congruent.

HQ = HL + LQ || Segment Addition Postulate: when L is between H and Q, the two parts add up to the whole. H Q equals H L plus L Q.
= 8 + 25 || H L is 8 and L Q is 25.
= 33 || So H Q is 33.

@@ g12Add 1002
kind: ## \w+ = \d+\. Find \w+\.$
title: Segment Addition: find a part
MP = MN + NP || The whole segment M P is made of the parts M N and N P.
35 = MN + 19 || M P is 35 and N P is 19.
MN = 35 − 19 = 16 || Subtract 19 from both sides: M N is 16.

@@ g12Add 1003
kind: Find the value of x
title: Segment Addition with x
EF + FG = EG || The two parts add up to the whole: E F plus F G equals E G.
(6x − 12) + (5x + 2) = 45 || Put in the expressions and the total, 45.
11x − 10 = 45 || Combine like terms: 6 x plus 5 x is 11 x, and negative 12 plus 2 is negative 10.
11x = 55 → x = 5 || Add 10: 11 x is 55. Divide by 11: x is 5.
EF = 6(5) − 12 = 18,  FG = 5(5) + 2 = 27 || Put 5 back in: E F is 30 minus 12, which is 18. F G is 25 plus 2, which is 27.
Check: 18 + 27 = 45 ✓ || Check: 18 plus 27 is 45.

@@ g12Challenge 1003
title: Challenge: Segment Addition with three expressions
AB + BC = AC || B is on segment A C, so A B plus B C equals A C.
(3x + 9) + (6x + 2) = 6x + 26 || Put in all three expressions.
9x + 11 = 6x + 26 || Combine like terms on the left.
3x = 15 → x = 5 || Subtract 6 x and 11 from both sides: 3 x is 15, so x is 5.
AC = 6(5) + 26 = 56 || The question also asks for A C: 30 plus 26 is 56.
