title: The Power Rule
d/dx xⁿ = nxⁿ⁻¹ || Power Rule: bring the exponent down in front, then lower the exponent by 1.
f′(x) = 15x⁴ − 2x − 5 || 3 x to the 5th becomes 15 x to the 4th. Minus x squared becomes minus 2 x. Minus 5 x becomes minus 5.
f′(1) = 15 − 2 − 5 = 8 || Put in 1: 15 minus 2 minus 5 is 8.

horizontal tangent ↔ f′(x) = 0 || A horizontal line has slope 0, so set the derivative equal to 0.
f′(x) = 3x² + 6x − 24 = 3(x² + 2x − 8) || Differentiate, then factor out 3.

x = −4 and x = 2 || So the tangent line is horizontal at x equals negative 4 and x equals 2.
