title: Limit of a sequence with a square root
√(9n² + 1)/(n + 4) || The top behaves like 3 n and the bottom like n for large n.
= √(9 + 1/n²)/(1 + 4/n) || Divide top and bottom by n. Inside the root, n is root n squared.
→ √9/1 = 3 || As n goes to infinity, 1 over n squared and 4 over n go to zero.

title: Repeating decimal with a non-repeating part
0.87̅ = 8/10 + 7/100 + 7/1000 + ⋯ || Split off the digit that does not repeat.
7/100 + 7/1000 + ⋯ : a = 7/100, r = 1/10 → (7/100)/(9/10) = 7/90 || The rest is geometric. Use a over 1 minus r.
