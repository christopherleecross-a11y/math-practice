title: Approximate a square root with a tangent line
f(x) = √x: f(81) = 9, f′(x) = 1/(2√x) → f′(81) = 1/18 || Use a perfect square near 84: 81. f of 81 is 9, and the slope there is 1 over 18.
L(x) = 9 + (1/18)(x − 81) || The tangent line is the linear approximation.
L(84) = 9 + 3/18 = 9 + 1/6 ≈ 9.167 || Put in 84: 9 plus one sixth, about 9.167.
