kind: square pyramid
title: Volume of a square pyramid
V = ⅓Bh || A pyramid's volume is one third of the base area times the height: one third of a prism with the same base and height.
B = 11 · 11 = 121 || The base is an 11 by 11 square: 121.
V = ⅓(121)(18) = ⅓(2178) = 726 yd³ || 121 times 18 is 2178. One third of that is 726 cubic yards.
