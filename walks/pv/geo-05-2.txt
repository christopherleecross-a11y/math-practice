match angles with the same marks || In congruent figures, matching angles have the same arc marks. Pair them up.
∠C ↔ ∠F,  ∠H ↔ ∠Y,  ∠J ↔ ∠W,  ∠X ↔ ∠S || The marks pair C with F, H with Y, J with W, and X with S.
CHJX ≅ FYWS || So C H J X is congruent to F Y W S, with the letters in matching order.
J̅X̅ ↔ W̅S̅ || Sides match by their endpoints. Side J X matches side W S, because J goes with W and X goes with S.
W̅S̅ || So W S is congruent to J X. Check each answer choice by matching its endpoints this way.

@@ g52AllPairs 1001
title: All pairs of corresponding parts
△BCD ≅ △TVU: B ↔ T, C ↔ V, D ↔ U || Pair up the vertices in order.
angles: ∠B ≅ ∠T, ∠C ≅ ∠V, ∠D ≅ ∠U || Each pair of matching vertices gives a pair of congruent angles.

@@ g52Rewrite 1001
title: Rewrite a congruence statement
B ↔ T, C ↔ V, D ↔ U || The pairs must stay together: B with T, C with V, D with U.
reorder both the same way: D B C → U T V || You can list the vertices in a different order, as long as you change both sides the same way.
△DBC ≅ △UTV || So triangle D B C is congruent to triangle U T V. For polygons, the letters also have to stay in order around the shape.
