title: Triangle Proportionality Theorem
P̅Q̅ ∥ M̅N̅ → it splits the sides proportionally || A segment parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally.
LP/PM = LQ/QN || On one side, L P over P M. On the other side, L Q over Q N. They're equal.
5/10 = x/26 || L P is 5, P M is 10, L Q is x, and Q N is 26.
x = 26 · 5/10 = 13 || Multiply by 26: x is 13.

title: Three Parallel Lines Theorem
three parallel lines split transversals proportionally || When three parallel lines cross two transversals, they cut the transversals into proportional pieces.
18/4 = 9/x || The pieces on one transversal are 18 and 4. The matching pieces on the other are 9 and x.
x = 4 · 9/18 = 2 || 9 is half of 18, so x is half of 4: 2.

@@ g84Bisector 1000
title: Triangle Angle Bisector Theorem
angle bisector → splits the opposite side in the ratio of the other two sides || An angle bisector divides the opposite side into pieces proportional to the two sides next to the angle.
NW/WQ = GN/GQ || The piece next to N over the piece next to Q equals side G N over side G Q.
5/x = 8/12 || N W is 5, W Q is x, G N is 8, and G Q is 12.
8x = 60 → x = 7.5 || Cross multiply: 8 x is 60, so x is 7.5.
