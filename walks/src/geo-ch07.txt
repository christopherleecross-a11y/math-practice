interior angle sum = (n − 2) · 180° || Example 1. The sum of the interior angles of a convex polygon with n sides is n minus 2, times 180 degrees. The polygon splits into n minus 2 triangles.
n = 12 || A 12-gon has 12 sides, so n is 12.
(12 − 2) · 180° = 10 · 180° || 12 minus 2 is 10, so that is 10 triangles of 180 degrees each.
= 1800° || 10 times 180 is 1800. The angles add up to 1800 degrees.

@@ geo-7-2 | Example 1
Opposite sides are congruent: AB = DC || Example 1. In a parallelogram, opposite sides are congruent. A B and D C are opposite sides.
3x − 4 = 2x + 5, so x = 9 || Set them equal and solve: subtract 2 x and add 4. x equals 9.
AB = 3(9) − 4 = 23 || A B is 3 times 9 minus 4, which is 23.
Consecutive angles are supplementary: m∠B = 180° − 70° = 110° || Angles A and B are consecutive angles, next to each other along a side. They add to 180, so angle B is 110 degrees.
Opposite angles are congruent: m∠C = m∠A = 70° || Angle C is opposite angle A, so it is also 70 degrees.
Answer: x = 9, AB = 23, m∠B = 110°, m∠C = 70° || So x is 9, A B is 23, angle B is 110 degrees, and angle C is 70 degrees.

m∠Q = 2(25) + 20 = 70° || Angle Q is 2 times 25 plus 20, which is 70 degrees. Check: 110 plus 70 is 180.

midpoint of J̅L̅: ((−1 + 5)/2, (2 + 0)/2) = (2, 1) || Find the midpoint of J L: negative 1 plus 5 over 2 is 2, and 2 plus 0 over 2 is 1. The diagonals meet at 2 comma 1.

@@ geo-7-3 | Example 1
Both pairs of opposite sides must be congruent. || Example 1. If both pairs of opposite sides of a quadrilateral are congruent, it is a parallelogram.
2x + 3 = x + 8, so x = 5 || A B and D C are opposite: 2 x plus 3 equals x plus 8. Subtract x and 3: x equals 5.
3y = y + 10, so y = 5 || A D and B C are opposite: 3 y equals y plus 10. Subtract y: 2 y equals 10, so y equals 5.
Answer: x = 5, y = 5 || When x is 5 and y is 5, A B and D C are both 13, and A D and B C are both 15. So A B C D is a parallelogram.

@@ geo-7-3 | Example 3
Parallel sides have equal slopes. || Example 3. One way to show a parallelogram: both pairs of opposite sides are parallel. Compare slopes.
slope A̅B̅ = (4 − 3)/(1 − (−3)) = 1/4 || Slope of A B: 4 minus 3 is 1, over 1 minus negative 3, which is 4. One fourth.
slope D̅C̅ = (0 − (−1))/(3 − (−1)) = 1/4 || Slope of D C: 0 minus negative 1 is 1, over 3 minus negative 1, which is 4. Also one fourth. So A B is parallel to D C.
slope A̅D̅ = (−1 − 3)/(−1 − (−3)) = −4/2 = −2 || Slope of A D: negative 1 minus 3 is negative 4, over negative 1 minus negative 3, which is 2. That is negative 2.
slope B̅C̅ = (0 − 4)/(3 − 1) = −4/2 = −2 || Slope of B C: 0 minus 4 is negative 4, over 3 minus 1, which is 2. Also negative 2. So A D is parallel to B C.
ABCD is a parallelogram. || Both pairs of opposite sides are parallel, so A B C D is a parallelogram.

m∠B = m∠A = 64° || Angles A and B share base A B, so angle B is also 64 degrees.
m∠D = 180° − 64° = 116° || Angles A and D are on the same leg, between the parallel bases, so they are supplementary. Angle D is 180 minus 64, which is 116 degrees.
m∠C = m∠D = 116° || Angles C and D share base C D, so angle C is also 116 degrees.
Check: 64 + 64 + 116 + 116 = 360 ✓ || Check: the four angles add to 360. Angle B is 64, and angles C and D are each 116 degrees.
